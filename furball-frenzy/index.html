How to play

Drop onto the island with up to 19 other critters. Grab weapons, smash crates, hide in bushes, build walls, and stay inside the safe circle. The storm shrinks every phase. Be the last critter standing to win!

Keyboard + mouse
WASD / arrows: move
Mouse: aim · Hold click: fire
Space: special ability
Q: build a wall (10 wood)
G: hold to aim a grenade, release to throw
E: pick up / swap weapon
R: reload · 123 / wheel: switch weapon
M: mute · P/Esc: pause
Touch (phone / tablet)
Left thumb: drag anywhere on the left half to move
Right thumb: drag on the right half to aim. It fires automatically while you drag.
Buttons: Ability, Build, Bomb (grenade, thrown the way you face), Grab, Reload
Tap a weapon slot to switch
Landscape works best!

Tips: Hit trees and crates with your paw for wood. Gold = Legendary, purple = Epic, blue = Rare. Bushes hide you until you shoot. Water slows you down. Grenades bounce, then blow up walls, crates and critters.

Play with a Friend: one player taps Host a Game and shares the 5-letter code or invite link; the other taps Join and types it. With Team up on you can't hurt each other and win together. Online play needs internet; both players should keep the game open in the front tab.
